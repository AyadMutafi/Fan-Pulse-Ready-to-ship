## PART 5 — Label fix (frontend)

In `src/app/page.tsx` the match label was rendered as `WC Group ${m.group}` for ALL WC matches.
This produced "WC Group R32" for knockout matches — nonsensical (R32 is a knockout round, not a
group).

**Fix:** render the label based on the `group` value:
- Single letter A-L → `WC Group {letter}`
- `R32` → `WC Round of 32`
- `R16` → `WC Round of 16`
- `QF` → `WC Quarter Finals`
- `SF` → `WC Semi Finals`
- `Final` → `WC Final`
- empty/other → `World Cup 2026`
