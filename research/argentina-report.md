## 7. Where Argentina Fell Short — 3-Bullet Trend Summary

1. **Messi went from tournament-GOAT to anonymous in the Final.** Lionel Messi entered the Final as Sofascore's highest-rated player of WC 2026 (9.03 average, 8 goals, 4 assists, broke Klose's all-time WC scoring record) and was leading the Golden Boot race. In the Final he posted an ESPN **3/10** — "had little impact as Argentina barely approached Spain's goal in a one-sided World Cup final" — losing the Golden Boot to Mbappé in the process. ESPN FC crew called Argentina's performance "a complete and utter disgrace"; Messi's "last dance" ended in "a meek 1-0 extratime defeat" (ESPN). The contrast between Messi's tournament body of work and his Final non-performance is the defining story of Argentina's runner-up finish.

2. **Defensive reshuffle + benching of veterans exposed the back line.** Scaloni made 3 changes for the Final (Montiel, Nico González, De Paul in), which meant the SF back four (likely Molina-Acuña or similar) was replaced by Montiel-Romero-Lisandro Martínez-Tagliafico. The reshuffled back line held Spain to 0-0 through 90 minutes only because Emi Martínez posted a Sofascore **9.6 with 11 saves (all-time WC Final record, 1.76 goals prevented)** — the keeper single-handedly kept Argentina alive. Once Enzo Fernández was sent off in the 93' (second yellow, challenge on Cubarsí) for 10-man Argentina, the dam broke: Ferran Torres scored the 106' AET winner. The combination of (a) a reshuffled defense that gave up chance after chance, (b) a red card in stoppage time, and (c) zero attacking output (Messi 3/10, Lautaro 4/10 "disappearing act" off the bench, Álvarez 5/10) meant Argentina produced one of the most passive Final performances in living memory despite a tournament-best 1.94 xG on paper.

3. **Argentina's attacking depth failed to fire when it mattered — and the Di María era was already over.** Argentina's WC 2026 attack was built around Messi (8 goals in the tournament) + Julián Álvarez (QF "screamer" vs Switzerland) + Lautaro Martínez (SF 90+2' winner). But in the Final, only Messi + Álvarez + Nico González started; Lautaro was benched and anonymous when he came on (4/10). Crucially, **Ángel Di María — the 2022 Final goalscorer and Argentina's big-game deliverer for a decade — was not even at the tournament**, having retired from international football after Copa América 2024. Fans openly mourned his absence ("the unsung hero of the 2022 final, and the missed saviour of the 2026 final"; "Di Maria should be in this World Cup to save Argentina from losing to Spain"). Without Di María's big-game menace and with Messi finally looking his age (39, per IG "MESSI 39 · YAMAL 19"), Argentina had no Plan B when Spain suffocated Messi — and the title defense ended not with a fight but with a whimper.
